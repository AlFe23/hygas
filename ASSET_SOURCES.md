## Scientific meaning and display units

- `hygas-graphical-abstract.png`: current homepage hero, copied unchanged from the user-selected `Slide2.PNG`. White background, published CH₄ framework, original figure labels and scales retained.

- `hygas-graphical-abstract.jpg`: exact image used in the repository README, preserved as the previous homepage asset. It illustrates the published CH₄ framework. Its historical ppb/concentration labels and all original pixels are preserved; it is not a CO₂ validation figure.
- `plume-uncertainty.png`: the editable-proof PNG corresponding to published Figure 5. The final paper identifies the panels as enhancement ΔX, instrument-only uncertainty σ_RMN, and total 1-σ uncertainty σ_tot; the display unit is ppm·m. It explains that total uncertainty combines instrument noise and a scene-background clutter term. The retrieval CLI produces the instrument term; the complete background/IME/flux analysis belongs to the downstream workflow.
- `background-matching.png`: IGARSS HyGAS presentation, slide 8. The central panel is spectral distance in z-score units. It is not a methane-enhancement map.
- `turkmenistan-enmap-enhancement.png` and `turkmenistan-prisma-enhancement.png`: original Poster1 panels in ppm·m, with plume contours and separate colour scales. `turkmenistan-ghgsat-enhancement.png` preserves the provider panel in ppb. Matching colours or tick values across these panels are not an equivalence of enhancement values.
- Buenos Aires January panels come from editable-proof media `image22.png`, `image23.png`, and `image24.png`, associated with published Figure 17. They show EnMAP, GHGSat, and EMIT enhancement/uncertainty and plume contours, respectively.
- Pakistan panels come from editable-proof media `image33.png` and `image34.png`, associated with published Figure 21. They show EMIT and GHGSat enhancement/uncertainty with plume contours.
- Revisit panels come from editable-proof media `image35.png` and `image37.png`, associated with the top rows of published Figures 22 and 23. They show column-wise EnMAP and Tanager-1 configurations. The scene-wide alternative remains available in the paper and pinned notebooks.
- The archived case-study paper panels above display derived enhancement and uncertainty in ppb. The notebook workflow contains a ppm·m-to-ppb conversion (`scripts/plumes_analyzer.py`, `ppm_m_to_ppb`). Their existing pixels, labels and scales are retained. The website performs no scientific unit conversion and does not rename ppb values as ppm·m.
- `framework-engine.png` is a conceptual illustration from Poster1, not a quantitative scientific product.

Methane 2026, 5(1), 10. Published 13 February 2026. [DOI: 10.3390/methane5010010](https://doi.org/10.3390/methane5010010).
